The Evidence Base

Sixteen safety sources, the standards referenced, the calculation assumptions and the limitations of a third-party recomputation.

01Safety Evidence Matrix

Sources are listed by type and credibility with the claim each supports. Regulator documents and standards carry the most weight.

02Standards Referenced

SAE ARP4754A and ARP4761, RTCA DO-178C/254/160G, ISO 26262, ISO 21448, ISO/SAE 21434 and EASA SC-VTOL-01.

03Calculation Assumptions

620 kg take-off mass, 16 rotors at 500 N, 40.8 kWh baseline sortie energy and a 20 g crash-tolerance reference.

04Limitations

No internal safety case, no failure-rate data, no inference from undisclosed configurations, and no authority conclusion.

05Chapter Summary

This is an independent recomputation, not an airworthiness finding. Every estimate states its assumptions and every unknown is marked as undisclosed.
