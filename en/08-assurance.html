An Argument That Can Be Challenged

GSN splits the case into four goals that must all hold; one broken chain collapses the top-level claim.

01GSN Argument Structure

Goal, strategy, four sub-goals and four evidence sets. Hazards identified, architecture meets targets, requirements verified, operations controlled.

02Four Evidence Chains

Formally complete, but not equally verifiable from outside. This is normal for a third-party review and is marked throughout.

03What Each Certificate Proves

TC covers design, AC the individual aircraft, PC production consistency, OC the operator. Four certificates distribute responsibility across four parties.

04Three Gaps in the Argument

Are the three flight controllers heterogeneous? What are the quantified crashworthiness limits? What is the remote-crew ratio and takeover time?

05Residual Risk & Acceptance

Weather, multi-arm failure, latent software defects, third-party ground risk and cyberattack are accepted under ODD limits and operating controls.

06Chapter Summary

Release level R2: valid inside the ODD, unargued outside it. Pointing out gaps is not a challenge to certification; it marks where extension would be weakest.
