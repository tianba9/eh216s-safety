Hardware by Isolation, Software by Constraint

Hardware faults are random and statistical; software defects are deterministic and repeat across all three channels.

01Hardware Failure Modes & Protection

Motors, ESCs, rotors, structure, batteries and sensors each have detection and isolation paths. A broken blade is the exception: nothing in software can repair it in flight.

02Single-Point & Latent Faults

SPFM and LFM matter more than the number of backups. A dormant fault in the standby channel appears only when that channel is needed.

03Software Safety Design

Requirements traceability, defensive programming, watchdogs, independent verification. Redundancy cannot fix a shared defect.

04Perception & SOTIF

Thin wires, glare, rain, non-cooperative aircraft and bird strikes cannot be solved by adding sensors alone; the answer is a narrower ODD.

05Remote-Crew Human Factors

Vigilance decay, takeover delay and multi-aircraft workload. The human is part of the control loop and must be designed for.

06Chapter Summary

Hardware safety works by detect–isolate–degrade; software safety works by process discipline. The two cannot borrow each other's methods.
