Valid Inside the ODD, Unargued Outside

Safety stands on two legs: an aircraft that can still land after failure, and an operating envelope that keeps it out of trouble.

01One Thread: Responsibility First

Define responsibility, then the mission, then hazards, architecture, hardware, software, verification and the final argument.

02Three Key Findings

Removing the pilot is the first architectural choice. Redundancy does not solve common causes. The safety boundary is the operating boundary.

03Three Undisclosed Gaps

Heterogeneity of the flight controllers, quantified crashworthiness, and remote-crew ratio and takeover time.

04Final Position

Design controls how safe it is when something goes wrong; operating limits control how often that happens. The second is easier to overlook and easier to erode.
